Magnetic tiles OEM: MOQ, samples & inspection

Start an OEM project with a quantity per SKU and a clear reason for the sample. A stock sample can help you review an existing product, while color or packaging samples address changes for your brand. Record what each sample is intended to demonstrate before using it to approve an order.

Discuss your projectExplore OEM options

1 · Understand what the MOQ applies to

Custom and private-label magnetic tile orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Special packaging, dedicated accessories or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ, confirmed during project review. For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Tell us which purchasing route you intend to use so the quotation can address the correct product and packaging scope.

Prepare a line for each model, version, colorway and quantity. Mixed-SKU requests are reviewed separately; 500 sets in total does not automatically qualify for mixed-SKU production. If you want several versions, list each one instead of giving only an assortment total. Ask how your requested colors and packaging affect the confirmed MOQ and keep the answer with the quotation revision.

2 · Define the sample you need

Say whether you want to examine an existing configuration or review proposed colors, packaging or selected accessories. For each sample, record the SKU, version, component list and any areas that remain provisional. A sample of the existing product can help you choose a direction; a changed box or component mix needs its own agreed review before it becomes the order reference.

Standard samples are normally charged, with international courier paid by the buyer. New development is reviewed separately. Request the applicable sample charges, courier arrangements and schedule with your brief. The next step depends on completed requirements, artwork, payment and necessary approvals, so confirm the starting point as well as the proposed sample date. Identify any test or document review needed alongside the sample.

3 · Use one sample review record

Keep photographs and comments under one sample reference. The following is a buyer preparation checklist to agree with the project team. It gives your colleagues a consistent way to describe observations and request changes; the final acceptance criteria and inspection scope are agreed for the selected product.

  • Identity: model, colorway, sample date, artwork revision and intended market.
  • Contents: component names and quantities, accessories, instructions and the arrangement inside the box.
  • Build and connections: assemble the proposed play route or structure and record the components used together.
  • Appearance: record comments on finish, printed artwork, colors and packaging with clear photos.
  • Decisions: identify approved items, requested changes, open questions and the person responsible for confirmation.

4 · Separate sample approval from shipment inspection

Agree the order specification, inspection scope and order terms before production and shipment arrangements are confirmed. If your team plans a third-party inspection, include the proposed timing, checks, records and any packing or photo requests in the project brief. Ask the project team to confirm the arrangements for the order. Inspection limits, sampling methods and acceptance decisions should be recorded in the agreed plan.

Ask for the documentation relevant to the exact product and destination. Third-party test reports available on request. Keep the document review alongside the approved product reference and artwork, so a change to configuration or packaging can be checked before it moves forward. The factory overview shows the documented dimension, weight and packing operations that you can discuss during planning.

5 · Move from approval to a confirmed order

A practical sequence is to share the product and market brief, select configuration and branding, review feasibility and quotation terms, approve samples and artwork, then confirm order terms and shipment arrangements. At each stage, identify what is still open. For new tooling or structures, ask for a separate review covering development, testing, commercial terms and IP scope.

Send your preferred SKU, quantity per color, destination, packaging brief, sample purpose and requested launch timing. Request a project-specific schedule after the scope is clear; testing and project changes can affect timing. Use the packaging guide to organize the files your designer will provide, then send your project details for review.
