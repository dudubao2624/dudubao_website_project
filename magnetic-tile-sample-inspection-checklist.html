How to check magnetic tile samples in 10 minutes

Use this magnetic tiles quality testing checklist for a first look at a received sample. Record what you see before discussing changes or approving a reference sample. This is a quick buyer screen; it cannot establish durability, magnet retention or market compliance.

Download the A4 checklistStart the 10-minute check

Adults only; keep children away. Stop if you find cracked or open shells, exposed or loose magnets, or fragments. Isolate the sample and keep all parts together. Do not pry, cut, drop, bend or pull parts to failure.

0:00-1:00

Prepare and identify

Retailers reviewing C01-C can add the LED track car set supplier sample review to check the listed 99-piece configuration, current LED track car and route instructions.

Use a clear, well-lit table, a phone camera and the supplied instructions. Have the specified batteries and a suitable screwdriver only if the instructions require them. Record SKU, sample reference, date, market and packing-list version. Pick examples of each supplied shape and joint; record which pieces you check. Ten minutes will not cover every piece in a large set. For the component layout and packing-list review, see how this step is handled.

1:00-3:00

Cracks and shell openings

Check: Turn each selected piece under the light. Look at corners, edges, seams and visible fasteners; use a phone close-up. Do not flex or open the shell. For the background to molded shell shapes and edges, see how this step is handled.

Clear screen: No visible cracks, open seams, sharp broken edges or visibly missing fasteners compared with the agreed reference.

Ask the factory: Which joints and fasteners belong on each shape, and can you provide a matching close-up?

3:00-5:00

Exposed or loose magnets

Check: Look through transparent areas and around edges. Check the tray for loose magnets or fragments. Do not retrieve a magnet by opening a tile. If anything is exposed or loose, stop and isolate.

Clear screen: No exposed magnets, loose magnets or visible fragments. Sound alone cannot confirm a defect or secure retention.

Ask the factory: What movement or sound is expected for this shape, and what should trigger a hold? For the magnet insertion and welding background, see how this step is handled.

5:00-6:30

Weak or inconsistent holding

Check: Join equal-size tile pairs from the same sample in the instructed orientation on the table. Repeat with another pair and make the same small freestanding build. Compare only like-for-like pieces.

Clear screen: Connections engage consistently and the chosen build stays together at rest. Record unexplained differences; this does not measure magnetic force.

Ask the factory: Which reference build and holding criteria should we agree for this SKU?

6:30-8:00

Loose track or tube joints

Check: If supplied, assemble a short supported route as instructed. Disconnect and reconnect one joint normally, then run the supplied ball or car through it. Stop if a tab cracks; do not force the fit.

Clear screen: Joints seat as instructed, stay connected during the run and let the ball or car pass without catching. Mark N/A if no track or tubes are supplied.

Ask the factory: Which connectors and supports belong here, and what fit should the agreed route show?

8:00-9:00

Cars that do not move

Check: If supplied, first confirm manual or powered operation. For a powered car, follow the instructions for the specified batteries, polarity, switch and secured cover. Try a short level route. Stop for heat, leakage or damage; do not open the motor housing.

Clear screen: A manual car rolls with a gentle push; a powered car starts and runs as instructed. Record stalls. Mark N/A if no car is supplied.

Ask the factory: What power source, setup and expected movement apply to this exact car?

9:00-10:00

Record and decide

Photograph concerns and mark the affected pieces. Record checked piece IDs, the build or route used and any unexamined items. Choose: continue review, hold for clarification, or stop and isolate. A clear screen is not order approval. Review the documentation against your selected SKU and target market.

Know which construction you are reviewing

For DUDUBAO D01-C and D10-C, the product sheets describe rivet reinforcement and ultrasonic sealing. These are construction descriptions; confirm the shapes represented by your sample. The plastic bodies of D01-C and D10-C are ABS; for A01-T and A03-T, ABS is documented for the transparent tubes, and for C01-C for the track pieces and LED track car. Do not extend these material descriptions to other parts.

Sample enquiry & review FAQ

What information do you need to review a sample enquiry?

Please share the exact SKU, sample quantity, evaluation purpose, target market, packaging needs and required date. Distinguish the sample preparation date from the required arrival date.

What should I check when evaluating a product sample?

Review the model, contents, colors, packaging and requested revisions in one record. Confirm the final contents with the selected sample and corresponding packaging before putting the piece count on your branded box.
