Wholesale sourcing FAQ

Can I order small quantities of DUDUBAO-branded stock?

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, we will check inventory and order feasibility for your proposed assortment. Please share the selected SKUs and quantities per SKU.

Can quantities from different SKUs be combined to meet the MOQ?

For custom and private-label magnetic tile orders, the minimum is 500 sets per SKU. This applies separately to each SKU. Quantities from different SKUs cannot be combined to meet the minimum.

What do you need for a wholesale quotation?

Send the SKU, quantity per SKU, target market, destination, brand-stock or private-label preference, packaging, sample and documentation requirements.

How long do samples and production take?

In-stock samples take about 7 days; custom color or packaging samples take about 15 days. These are preparation times, excluding transit. Sample arrangements are confirmed per project. Typical production takes 30–40 days after sample and order details are confirmed. Testing may affect timing.

How do I check safety documentation?

Start with the exact stock SKU and configuration you plan to order. Use the safety testing guide to compare the sample identity, component list and destination requirements before relying on a document. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.

What retail-box dimensions are recorded for the five main sets?

Recorded retail-box dimensions are D01-C: 322 × 240 × 73 mm; D10-C: 510 × 330 × 100 mm; A01-T: 484 × 410 × 105 mm; A03-T: 370 × 330 × 90 mm; C01-C: 320 × 270 × 74 mm. These are reference values, not current-batch measurements. Confirm the sample and corresponding packaging.

What single-box weights are recorded for the five main sets?

Recorded single-box weights are D01-C: 3.25 kg; D10-C: 8 kg; A01-T: 3.25 kg; A03-T: 1.89 kg; C01-C: 2.2 kg. These are box-weight references, not single-set net weights or current-batch measurements. Confirm the sample and corresponding packaging.

Are the packing references a packing list for our current shipment?

Packing data are reference values from product records, not measurements of the current batch. Confirm the sample, corresponding packaging and final packing details for your order.
