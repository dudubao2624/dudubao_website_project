Wholesale sourcing FAQ

Can I order DUDUBAO stock or private label?

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Special packaging, dedicated accessories, or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ, confirmed during project review.

Can I mix colors or SKUs?

List quantity per SKU and colorway. Mixed-SKU requests are reviewed separately; 500 sets in total does not automatically qualify for mixed-SKU production.

What do you need for a wholesale quotation?

Send the SKU, quantity per SKU, target market, destination, brand-stock or private-label preference, packaging, sample and documentation requirements.

How do I check safety documentation?

Third-party test reports (EN71, AS/NZS) available on request. Share your selected product and destination with your request.
