Plan quantities around classroom activities

For education distributors and school suppliers serving Europe, a useful brief starts with the intended age group, activity, number of simultaneous groups and time available for setup and collection. Choose the proposed build, list the components it uses and try it with the selected sample. Use that review to decide how many sets each group needs.

Calculate classroom quantities as simultaneous groups × reviewed sets per group, then list any replenishment stock separately. Keep the component mix in view: a total piece count includes listed accessories and is not a promise of how many pupils a set will serve. Align intended ages and supervision instructions with the selected product and project documents.

Make storage part of the project brief

Describe the storage format your school channel needs: one kit per group or a shared collection, sorting by shape or function, a contents list and a return check after each activity. Review container dimensions, component access, labels and packaging with the selected sample. Storage formats and replacement arrangements are project discussion items; confirm their scope before placing an order.

Develop activity cards with the confirmed set

Bring draft activity-card content for review alongside the product sample. Possible prompts include copying a shape, building a bridge, comparing ball-run routes or assembling a car-track layout. Each draft card should identify the required components, illustrated steps, a discussion prompt and the relevant supervision wording.

Confirm the instruction languages, artwork revision and contents match before developing a printed pack. Activity cards are a development option to discuss for the project; their inclusion and format need confirmation with the sample and packaging. Use the packaging and artwork guide to prepare the handover.

Plan MOQ, samples and production

Custom and private-label orders have an MOQ of 500 sets per SKU. In-stock samples take about 7 days; custom color or packaging samples take about 15 days. These are preparation times, excluding transit. Identify whether the sample is for selecting a set, trying a classroom activity or reviewing your proposed packaging and materials.

Mass production takes 30–40 days, starting after the sample and order details are confirmed. Record the selected set, quantities per SKU, packaging, instruction languages and revisions in one brief. Education distributor FAQ

How should we plan magnetic tile quantities for a classroom?

Start with the number of simultaneous activity groups, the selected build and the component list. Try the activity with the selected sample before deciding sets per group, then calculate classroom and replenishment quantities. Piece counts include listed accessories and do not by themselves determine a class size.

Can we discuss storage and activity cards for school supply projects?

Yes. Share your proposed storage format, sorting labels, instruction languages and activity-card content. These are project options for review, with contents, artwork and packaging confirmed against the selected sample.

What are the MOQ, sample and production timings?

Custom and private-label orders have an MOQ of 500 sets per SKU. In-stock samples take about 7 days; custom color or packaging samples take about 15 days. These are preparation times, excluding transit. Mass production takes 30–40 days, starting after the sample and order details are confirmed.
