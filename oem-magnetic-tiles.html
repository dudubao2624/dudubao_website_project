08 · BUYER FAQ

OEM Magnetic Tiles FAQ

01What customization options can we discuss?

Please specify your proposed private-label logo, colors, packaging, set configuration and existing accessory combinations. Feasibility and compatibility need confirmation for the selected set. New structures or tooling require separate review.

02What is the MOQ for custom and private-label orders?

Custom and private-label magnetic tile orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Please identify the selected SKU and quantity per SKU.

03How long does sample preparation take?

In-stock samples take about 7 days; custom color or packaging samples take about 15 days. These are preparation times, excluding transit. Confirm the selected SKU, sample scope and required date for your project.

04When does the production lead time start?

Production takes 30–40 days after sample approval and confirmation of order details. Please share the quantity per SKU and required date so the project schedule can be reviewed.

05Which safety reports can I review?

For your proposed private-label set, list the component and packaging revisions alongside the target market. Use the safety testing guide to identify the scope and sample references to check in any proposed report. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.

06What is the custom order process?

Share the product, market and quantity brief; select the configuration and branding; review feasibility, quotation and timing; approve samples, artwork and applicable documents; then confirm order terms, production review and shipment arrangements.

07What should I send for a quotation?

Send SKU and version, quantity per color, market, destination, brand-stock or private-label preference, packaging, sample and documentation needs. Mixed-SKU production is reviewed separately; 500 sets in total does not automatically qualify.
