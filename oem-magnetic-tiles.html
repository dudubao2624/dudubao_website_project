MOQ, Samples & Production Timing

01MOQ

Standard 500 sets

Trial orders from 300 sets may be reviewed for selected existing configurations with quantity-specific pricing. Special packaging, accessories, or customization may require a higher MOQ.

02Samples

~7 days in-stock; ~15 days for color/packaging

Standard samples are normally charged, and international courier costs are normally paid by the buyer. Timing starts after product requirements, artwork, payment, and necessary approvals are complete. Special development is quoted separately.

03Production

Typically 30–40 days; 40–50 days in peak periods

Timing starts after agreed payment and confirmation of product configuration, colors, packaging, and order details. Testing and project requirements may affect timing; new development and tooling are reviewed separately.

08 · BUYER FAQ

Common Questions About OEM Magnetic Tiles

01What is your MOQ?

Standard MOQ is 500 sets. Selected existing configurations may be reviewed from 300 sets at quantity-specific pricing; special projects may require more.

02Can I order samples, and how long do they take?

Samples can normally be arranged by project. Standard samples are charged, with international courier paid by the buyer. In-stock samples take about 7 days; color or packaging samples about 15 days, after necessary details and approvals are complete.

03Can I choose colors, existing shapes, and set configurations?

Colors, existing shapes, sets, and selected accessories can be discussed. Compatibility and availability are reviewed by product. New shapes or structures require separate evaluation.

04Can I use my own logo and packaging?

Private-label logos, packaging formats, and artwork can be reviewed. Please share your market, quantity, and packaging requirements.

05Can you develop new accessories or structures?

Such projects may be evaluated for feasibility, testing, quantity, commercial terms, tooling, and IP requirements.

06How long does production take?

Typically 30–40 days, or 40–50 days in peak periods, after agreed payment and required order details are confirmed. Complex customization is assessed separately.

07What testing or compliance documents are available?

Existing reports are reviewed for the specific product version and market. Requirements may include EN71, ASTM F963, CPSIA, AS/NZS, and REACH; coverage varies.

08What do you need for a quotation?

Share your market, product or set, quantity, packaging, customization, and testing needs. You can identify details that are not yet decided.
