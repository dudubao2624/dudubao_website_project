04 · PROJECT FIT

MOQ, Samples and Production Timing

01MOQ

Brand stock & new production

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Special packaging, dedicated accessories, or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ, confirmed during project review.

02Samples

~7 days in-stock; ~15 days for color/packaging

Standard samples are normally charged, and international courier costs are normally paid by the buyer. Timing starts after product requirements, artwork, payment, and necessary approvals are complete. Special development is quoted separately.

03Production

Typically 30–40 days; 40–50 days in peak periods

Timing starts after agreed payment and confirmation of product configuration, colors, packaging, and order details. Testing and project requirements may affect timing; new development and tooling are reviewed separately.

08 · BUYER FAQ

OEM Magnetic Tiles FAQ

01What can I customize for OEM magnetic tiles?

Discuss private-label logos, packaging, colors, set configuration and selected existing accessory combinations. Confirm feasibility and compatibility for the chosen set. New shapes, accessories, tooling and dedicated development projects need separate development, testing, commercial and IP review.

02What is the MOQ for private-label magnetic tiles?

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Special packaging, dedicated accessories, or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ, confirmed during project review.

03How long does sampling take and who pays?

In-stock samples typically take about 7 days; color or packaging samples about 15 days after requirements, artwork, payment and necessary approvals are complete. Standard samples are normally charged, with international courier paid by the buyer. New development is reviewed separately.

04What is the production lead time?

Typical production takes 30–40 days, or 40–50 days in peak periods, after agreed payment and confirmation of configuration, colors, packaging and order details. Testing may affect timing. Stock dispatch and new development are confirmed separately.

05Which safety reports can I review?

Third-party test reports (EN71, AS/NZS) available on request. Share your selected product and destination with your request.

06What is the custom order process?

Share the product, market and quantity brief; select the configuration and branding; review feasibility, quotation and timing; approve samples, artwork and applicable documents; then confirm order terms, production review and shipment arrangements.

07What should I send for a quotation?

Send SKU and version, quantity per color, market, destination, brand-stock or private-label preference, packaging, sample and documentation needs. Mixed-SKU production is reviewed separately; 500 sets in total does not automatically qualify.
