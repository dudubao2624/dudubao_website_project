A01-C and A01-T: choose the tube presentation

Both A01 configurations contain 119 pieces. A01-C uses colorful tubes, while A01-T uses clear tubes so the ball can be seen moving through the route. The existing selection information describes Y-shaped tubes, stairs and sloping pieces, with seven wooden balls for taking turns. Use the chosen version as the reference when discussing your component mix, colors and retail images.

For an importer building a tube-run assortment, compare the visual direction and the proposed box presentation with your own range. Ask for the packing list of each quoted version. Equal piece counts give a quick comparison point; a sourcing decision also needs the actual parts, sample build, packaging and order conditions. For A01-C, the current selection card is the existing model reference.

A03-T: a complete bell-play direction

A03-T is a complete 73-piece bell ball run. Its existing selection information lists six bells and five metal balls, with hole tiles, tubes and other route-building pieces. Rolling balls strike bells within the build. It is a direction to review when your brand wants route construction and audible bell play presented together in a complete set.

Review the full A03-T component list and build with the sample. Ask your designer to distinguish the supplied configuration from any larger illustrative structure. If you plan your own bell-play range, identify the palette, configuration, instructions and packaging changes together, then review feasibility and applicable documentation for that selected version and destination.

A05-C: make the expansion boundary clear

A05-C contains eight bells and two balls only. It does not include the tubes, magnetic tiles or supporting construction pieces shown in combined builds. A buyer considering an expansion offer should identify the supporting set or build, confirm the exact components used together and review the result with a sample. State that supporting-build requirement clearly in the product brief.

Keep expansion-pack photography and box contents easy to understand. Use a contents image for what ships in one A05-C pack, and identify extra construction pieces in any combined play illustration. Review ball, bell, tube and support fit for the proposed build with our team. Compatibility should be checked for the selected components and configuration before an expansion is added to your range.
