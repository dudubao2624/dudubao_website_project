A01-C 119-Piece Magnetic Marble Run

A01-C combines magnetic support tiles with tubes to build a ball run. Use straight sections, bends and branching pieces to change the route, then take turns releasing the seven wooden balls.

How to play

  • Build bends and branching routes with the tubes and hole tiles.
  • Rearrange Y-shaped tubes, stairs and slopes, then release a wooden ball to follow the new route.
  • Use the included bowls for ball-catching play. Market availability is confirmed for each project
