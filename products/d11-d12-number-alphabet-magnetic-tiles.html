FAQ

How do D11-C and D12 differ?

D11-C has numbers 0–9 and arithmetic symbols printed on both sides of its cards. D12-C and D12-M have 26 A–Z cards with uppercase and lowercase printing.

Are all 36 or 52 pieces magnetic tiles?

The counts include two component types. D11-C has 18 magnetic frames and 18 non-magnetic cards. Each D12 set has 26 magnetic frames and 26 non-magnetic cards.

Can the cards be changed?

Yes. The cards are replaceable, so you can change the number or letter displayed in a square frame.

Which versions are listed?

D11-C is the rainbow number set. D12-C and D12-M are the rainbow and pastel alphabet sets. D11-A is a separate 316-piece number-and-alphabet bundle.
