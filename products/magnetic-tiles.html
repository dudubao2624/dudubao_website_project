Product directions

Plan a range, not a single generic set.

The approved magnetic tile families cover distinct range-building directions. Exact SKU, piece count, materials, packaging, availability, and test coverage are confirmed for the selected version during project review.

  • Standard Magnetic TilesRainbow, pastel, and clear directions.
  • Mini Magnetic TilesA separate compact product-family direction; exact configuration is reviewed per project.
  • Themed Magnetic Tile SetsTheme-led product planning without assuming every theme or configuration is available.
  • Project customizationPrivate label, packaging, set configuration, color development, and selected existing accessory combinations.

Procurement information

  • MOQStandard MOQ is 500 sets. Trial orders from 300 sets may be evaluated for selected existing configurations with quantity-specific pricing.
  • SamplesSamples are available for qualified projects. Standard samples are normally charged, and international courier costs are normally paid by the buyer.
  • TimingTypical production lead time is 30–40 days, or 40–50 days during peak periods. Final timing depends on configuration, packaging, approvals, testing, and order details.
  • Compliance scopeApplicable test documentation is verified against the selected model, version, materials, packaging, and target market.

Some product versions, packaging formats, dedicated accessories, or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ. New structures, tooling, and exclusive programs are subject to feasibility, testing, commercial, and IP review.
