Wholesale Magnetic Tiles FAQ

Which magnetic tile sets can I compare for wholesale sourcing?

Start with D01-C, a 102-piece rainbow set, D10-C, a 219-piece rainbow set, and D01-M, a 102-piece pastel set. Confirm the selected version, packaging, and availability when requesting a quotation.

What is the MOQ for wholesale magnetic tiles?

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Special packaging, dedicated accessories, or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ, confirmed during project review.

Can I request samples before placing a wholesale order?

Samples are available for qualified projects. Standard samples are normally charged, and international courier costs are normally paid by the buyer. In-stock samples typically take about 7 days. Confirm availability and timing for your selected product.

What are the production lead times?

Typical production lead time is 30–40 days, or 40–50 days during peak periods. Final timing depends on configuration, packaging, approvals, testing requirements, and order details. Brand-stock availability and shipment timing are confirmed separately.

What should I send a magnetic tiles supplier for a quotation?

Share the SKU, quantity per SKU, target market, destination, packaging, branding, and testing needs. Applicable test documentation is reviewed against the selected model, version, materials, packaging, and market. Include any sample requirements with your brief.
