Bell Ball Run Questions

What is the Music Bell System direction?

It combines magnetic construction with bell elements for sound-led play. Discuss the intended configuration and market with DUDUBAO. The model, piece count, included components, packaging, and availability are confirmed for the selected version.

What is the MOQ for music magnetic systems?

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Music-system configurations, dedicated accessories, packaging, or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ, confirmed during project review.

Can I request private-label music magnetic systems?

Private label, packaging, set configuration, color development, and selected existing accessory combinations can be reviewed for the chosen product. New accessories, structures, tooling, and dedicated development projects can be evaluated subject to feasibility, testing, commercial, and IP review.

Can I request samples and a production schedule?

Samples may be available for qualified projects. Availability, sample type, fees, and timing are confirmed for the selected configuration. International courier costs are normally paid by the buyer. Typical production is 30–40 days, or 40–50 days during peak periods, subject to configuration, packaging, approvals, testing, and order details. Development timing is reviewed separately.
