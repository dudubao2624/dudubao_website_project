FAQ

What is the difference between D13-C and D13-M?

D13-C has 21 rainbow linked tiles, with seven of each length. D13-M has 24 pastel linked tiles, with eight of each length.

How is the piece count calculated?

Each two-, three- or four-square linked tile counts as one piece, giving 21 pieces for D13-C or 24 for D13-M.

What can I do with the different lengths?

Sort and compare the lengths, place them in rows or arrange them in a stepped display. Change the layout to explore different patterns and spans.

Do the linked tiles fold?

No. Each linked tile is a rigid one-piece length and does not fold.

Can D13 be used with standard magnetic tiles?

Yes. D13 linked tiles can be combined with standard DUDUBAO magnetic tiles in the same build.
