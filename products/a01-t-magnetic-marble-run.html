A01-T 119-Piece Magnetic Marble Run

Build a route and watch the ball move through clear tubes. A01-T combines magnetic support tiles, route sections and wooden balls for a magnetic marble run product direction.

The listed 119-piece configuration includes 30 square support tiles with circular openings and 7 wooden balls, with straight, curved, U-shaped and Y-shaped tubes, stairs and sloping sections. The listed accessories include 2 windmills and 2 bowls. Review the current packing list to distinguish this version from earlier sets.
