Buyer questions

A01-T sourcing FAQ

What is the MOQ for A01-T?

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU.

Can I review a sample and lead time?

In-stock samples take about 7 days; custom color or packaging samples take about 15 days. These are preparation times, excluding transit. Sample arrangements are confirmed per project. New development is reviewed separately. Typical production takes 30–40 days after sample and order details are confirmed. Testing may affect timing. Stock dispatch and new development are confirmed separately.

Can I order private label?

Share your logo, packaging artwork, selected configuration and quantity per SKU. Colors, packaging and existing accessory combinations are reviewed for feasibility. New structures and tooling need separate development, testing and IP review.

What test documentation is available?

We have EN 71 reports for selected samples; coverage of the current sets and target-market documentation is confirmed per project.

What should I confirm when reviewing an A01-T sample?

Please identify whether the sample is for choosing an existing set or reviewing proposed changes. Review the model, contents, colors, packaging and requested revisions in one record. Confirm the final contents with the selected sample and corresponding packaging before putting a piece count on your branded box.

Review magnetic tile shapes and edges against the selected sample: see how this step is handled. For assembled magnetic tile seams, see how this step is handled.

Confirm the component layout and corresponding packaging with the selected sample: see how this step is handled.

What artwork and box details should I prepare for A01-T packaging?

Please share your logo files, proposed box format, artwork, barcode information, target market and intended languages. Identify the required spaces for product identification, warnings and business details. Confirm the dieline and print revision against the selected set, then review the proposed box with a packaging sample.

How do I confirm instruction languages if the A01-T configuration changes?

Please share the proposed instruction languages, text and build illustrations. Check the component list, assembly steps and safety wording against the selected configuration. If the component mix changes, review the booklet and box together. Record the confirmed language and artwork versions before approving the sample.

How many A01-T sets should I plan for classroom groups?

Start with the number of simultaneous activity groups and the components needed for the planned build. Try the activity with the selected sample before deciding sets per group. Calculate classroom quantities as simultaneous groups multiplied by reviewed sets per group, with replenishment stock listed separately. Piece counts include listed accessories.
