Assortment fit & use

For buyers comparing track-play assortments

Consider C02-C for a range centered on track layouts and traffic scenes. Brands and distributors can compare the listed track, bridge and vehicle configuration with C01-C before selecting a retail set.

  1. Lay out the track

    Review straight, curved and slope sections with the magnetic bases. Check connections against the selected sample and instructions.

  2. Add the raised sections

    Review how the bridge pillars and decks support the proposed route. Place the traffic signs around the layout being assessed.

  3. Review the LED track cars

    Try the listed cars only as described in the current instructions. Confirm operation, power source and supplied items with the selected sample before preparing the retail instructions.
