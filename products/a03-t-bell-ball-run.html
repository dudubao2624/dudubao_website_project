Buyer questions

A03-T selection & private-label FAQ

What is included in the A03-T 73-piece set?

The listed configuration contains 19 square tiles with circular openings; 7 short straight tubes, 14 quarter-turn tubes, 3 U-shaped tubes and 1 disc; 5 iron balls, 6 bells, 6 bell support posts and 12 supports. The count includes these accessories. The instruction manual is listed separately.

How does A03-T differ from A01-T and A05-C?

A03-T is a complete 73-piece bell ball run with 6 bells and 5 iron balls. A01-T is a 119-piece clear-tube set with 7 wooden balls and 2 bowls. A05-C is a separate 10-piece expansion pack containing 8 bells and 2 balls; other construction pieces in combined builds are excluded.

What should I prepare for a private-label bulk order?

Start with A03-T, your quantity per model, target market, logo and packaging brief. Identify proposed changes to the listed contents, then review the sample, box artwork and instruction language together. Minimum quantities, sample arrangements and production timing are confirmed per project.

What should I check before approving the A03-T sample?

Check the contents against the 73-piece list, including the 5 iron balls, 6 bells and 6 bell support posts. Photos may show additional components, and colored balls in a photo do not establish the supplied ball material.
