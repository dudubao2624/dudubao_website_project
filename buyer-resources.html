05 / Sourcing FAQ

A few practical answers

For more buying questions, use the existing sourcing FAQ.

Read all sourcing FAQs →
What is the MOQ for custom or private-label sets?

Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU.

How long do samples and production take?

Allow about 7 days for in-stock samples or about 15 days for custom color or packaging samples. Production takes 30–40 days after the sample and order details are confirmed.

Can I download the PDFs directly?

Yes. The five buyer sheets and two printable tools above link directly to the existing PDF files.

How should I use the packing figures?

Packing figures are reference values from product records, not measurements of the current batch. Confirm the selected sample and corresponding packaging for each order.
