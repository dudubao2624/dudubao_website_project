Plan the combination

Match the parts to the play idea.

Range planning and physical connections are different decisions. Review the exact selected combination before developing packaging or instructions.

  • D01 and D10-C: core set choicesCompare a 102-piece direction with a 219-component configuration. Each is a set choice; D10-C does not require a D01 base set.
  • A01, A03 and A05: set or expansionA01 is a tube-run direction; A03-T is a complete bell set; A05-C is a separate bell-and-ball pack. Check ball, bell, tube and support fit for the proposed build.
  • Tracks and construction: separate branchesC01-C / C02-C vehicle tracks are distinct from tube ball runs. For B07-C, review the chosen magnetic and studded components within the construction build.
  • MINI: a separate formatKeep MINI dimensions and connectors separate from standard-size sets. Large-stud and small-stud components also need their own connection review.

Baseplates and long-span pieces such as D06 / D13 can be reviewed for a selected build. Shared photos do not confirm the contents, colors or connections of every SKU.

Before you choose

Questions about your range

Does every set connect to every other set?

Connections depend on the selected components and sizes. MINI, large-stud and small-stud products are different formats. Ask us to review the exact combination you plan to use.

Should my brand start with a complete set or an expansion?

Choose a complete set when the product needs its own building components. A03-T is a complete bell ball run; A05-C contains eight bells and two balls only. Confirm the supporting build and connections for an expansion offer.

Can I source DUDUBAO-branded products for distribution?

Small orders of DUDUBAO-branded stock are supported, subject to actual inventory. Review wholesale and stock-order options →

Which play direction fits your brand?

Send your target market, selected system, quantity per SKU and customization brief. We can review the parts platform and next sample step with you.
