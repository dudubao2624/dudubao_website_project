What buyers check

Three questions brand buyers ask about magnetic tiles

Review the selected set against its product records and an agreed inspection plan. Construction descriptions do not establish test results.

Magnets sealed inside the tile

Product sheets for D01-C, D10-C, A01-T, A03-T and C01-C describe ultrasonic sealing. D01-C and D10-C also list rivet reinforcement. These are manufacturing-process statements, not magnetic safety test results.

Connection strength

Verified magnet size, grade and pull-force values are not available in the supplied records for D01-C, D10-C, A01-T, A03-T and C01-C. Connection strength should not be used as a substitute for magnetic safety evidence.

Shell and seam durability

Review the selected sample and define the inspection scope. Product-sheet sealing descriptions do not demonstrate drop-test results or guarantee that magnets can never become exposed.

Testing & documentation FAQ

How can I verify that a test report applies to my magnetic tile order?

Review the complete report, including model annexes, sample photographs, results and amendments. Compare its model and component list with the proposed order, packaging revision and batch reference; ask for a written cross-reference where codes differ. Our confirmed piece counts are D01-C 102, D10-C 219, A01-T 119, A03-T 73 and C01-C 99; a matching count is only an identity check. Use the safety testing guide for the review fields. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.

What should I include when asking about test documentation?

Send the product code, target market, expected quantity and any retailer-specific documentation requirements.

Can I request product specifications?

Yes. Request the selected SKU and version. Only verified product-record fields should be used; C01-C outer-carton dimensions and gross weight remain unverified and are not quoted. Confirm the approved configuration and packaging before using reference specifications. Include your selected product code.

Can I request a sample before ordering?

In-stock samples take about 7 days; custom color or packaging samples take about 15 days. These are preparation times, excluding transit. Sample arrangements are confirmed per project. Include your selected product and delivery destination.

Can I use the manufacturer’s reports for my own-brand CPC?

Start with a list of the documents your importer or compliance reviewer needs for the proposed own-brand product. Put your brand, exact SKU, component list and packaging revision beside the sample identity and scope in any proposed supporting report. Record gaps and who will resolve them before treating that material as a basis for your CPC; a related model name alone is insufficient. Use the safety testing guide to prepare that comparison. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.

What evidence supports the magnetic safety of the selected set?

Product records for D01-C and D10-C describe ultrasonic sealing and rivet reinforcement. Records for A01-T, A03-T and C01-C describe ultrasonic sealing; rivet reinforcement for these three models has not been verified. These are recorded process descriptions, not a guarantee of magnet retention in the current batch.

Use the safety testing guide to organise the evidence review. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.

Does magnet size or strength change the safety assessment for my selected set?

Request a component-level magnet specification and the measurement method, then match each entry to the tile or accessory in your sample and any proposed test evidence. Record dimensions, material or grade, and how strength was measured as separate fields. Attraction during play cannot fill missing test data; keep unverified values open for review. Use the safety testing guide to distinguish magnet measurements from a general holding check. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.

What if the age range on a report differs from my planned packaging?

Place the proposed box, instructions and warnings beside the report’s sample description and stated age scope. List each difference, identify the product version behind each document and ask your reviewer to resolve the discrepancy before finalising the artwork. Do not transfer an age statement from another SKU or treat a catalogue recommendation as the decision for this packaging. Use the safety testing guide to keep the sample and packaging references together. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.
