What buyers check

Three questions brand buyers ask about magnetic tiles

Parents' reviews of magnetic tiles most often mention loose magnets, weak connections and cracked seams. Here is how we address each one.

Magnets sealed inside the tile

Magnets are inserted into the plastic shell, which is then closed by ultrasonic welding.

Connection strength

Builders need tiles that hold together in tall structures. We can share pull-force readings for the selected model with your sample, so you can compare against your own checks.

Shell and seam durability

Seams and edges should stay closed after normal drops. Review the welded seams on your sample and agree drop and visual checks in your inspection plan.

Testing & documentation FAQ

Which test reports can I request?

We hold EN71 and AS/NZS 8124 reports for selected models and components. Coverage is model-specific: tell us your product code and target market, and we will confirm which report applies or discuss testing for your configuration.

What should I include with my report request?

Send the product code, target market, expected quantity and any retailer-specific documentation requirements.

Can I request product specifications?

Yes. Materials, sets per carton, outer carton dimensions and carton weight are available on request. Include your selected product code.

Can I request a sample before ordering?

In-stock samples typically take about 7 days. Standard samples are normally charged, with international courier paid by the buyer. Include your selected product and delivery destination.
