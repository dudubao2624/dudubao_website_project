Testing & documentation FAQ

Which test reports can I request?

Third-party test reports (EN71, AS/NZS) available on request.

What should I include with my report request?

Send the product code, target market, expected quantity and any retailer-specific documentation requirements.

Can I request product specifications?

Yes. Materials, sets per carton, outer carton dimensions and carton weight are available on request. Include your selected product code.

Can I request a sample before ordering?

In-stock samples typically take about 7 days. Standard samples are normally charged, with international courier paid by the buyer. Include your selected product and delivery destination.
