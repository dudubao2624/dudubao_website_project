What Is Your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Custom and private-label magnetic tile orders start at 500 sets per SKU. Small-quantity orders for DUDUBAO branded stock are supported, subject to actual stock availability. Please share your selected models and quantities.

What Makes DUDUBAO Different From Other Magnetic Toy Suppliers?

DUDUBAO offers more than established magnetic tile products. We focus on original design, product innovation, and experienced quality control.

By combining magnetic toy development expertise with OEM/ODM capabilities, we help international toy brands create differentiated and competitive product ranges.

Our goal is to become a reliable long-term product partner, not simply fulfill individual orders.

Which magnetic-tile, tube-run and car-track sets are in your main range, and how many pieces are included?

The main range includes D01-C, a 102-piece magnetic-tile set; D10-C, a 219-piece magnetic-tile set; A01-T, a 119-piece transparent tube ball-run set; A03-T, a 73-piece bell ball-run set; and C01-C, a 99-piece LED car-track set. Counts include the listed non-tile accessories; manuals are listed separately. These are product-record specifications; confirm the sample and corresponding packaging for your order.

What plastic materials are used in the sets?

The plastic bodies of D01-C and D10-C are ABS. The transparent tubes in A01-T and A03-T are ABS, and the track pieces and LED track car in C01-C are ABS. These statements do not establish the materials of every accessory, coating, ball or bell.

What age grading is stated in your product records, and is the warning artwork approved for our destination?

Product records for D01-C, D10-C, A01-T, A03-T and C01-C suggest ages 3 and up. Final age grading and warning labels still need confirmation for the selected configuration and destination.

What are the recorded retail barcodes for D01-C, D10-C and the current 99-piece C01-C?

The recorded retail barcodes are D01-C: 6975318460003; D10-C: 6975318460355; C01-C, 99 pieces: 6975318461239. These refer to the recorded branded versions. Private-label barcode allocation needs separate confirmation.

What retail-box dimensions are recorded for D01-C, D10-C, A01-T, A03-T and C01-C?

Recorded retail-box dimensions are D01-C: 322 × 240 × 73 mm; D10-C: 510 × 330 × 100 mm; A01-T: 484 × 410 × 105 mm; A03-T: 370 × 330 × 90 mm; C01-C: 320 × 270 × 74 mm. These are reference values, not current-batch measurements; confirm the sample and corresponding packaging.

What master-carton quantities, dimensions and gross weights are recorded for D01-C, D10-C, A01-T, A03-T and C01-C?

Recorded master-carton data are: D01-C, 5 sets, 395 × 337 × 255 mm, 17.25 kg gross; D10-C, 2 sets, 525 × 345 × 230 mm, 17 kg gross; A03-T, 6 sets, 550 × 380 × 340 mm, 12.34 kg gross. Product records list an Instruction Book for A01-T and an Inspiration Book for D01-C, D10-C and C01-C. A printed guide for A03-T has not yet been verified. Guide languages, current supplied versions and teacher lesson plans still need confirmation.
