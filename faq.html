Products & specifications

Which magnetic-tile, ball-run and car-track sets are in your main range?

The main range includes D01-C, a 102-piece magnetic-tile set; D10-C, a 219-piece magnetic-tile set; A01-T, a 119-piece transparent tube ball-run set; A03-T, a 73-piece bell ball-run set; and C01-C, a 99-piece LED car-track set. Counts include listed accessories; manuals are separate. Confirm the sample and corresponding packaging.

Do the piece counts mean magnetic tiles only?

Piece counts include the listed non-tile accessories. Manuals are listed separately. Use the selected model’s component list and confirm the final contents with the sample and corresponding packaging.

Which parts of the main sets are ABS?

The plastic bodies of D01-C and D10-C are ABS. The transparent tubes of A01-T and A03-T are ABS. The track pieces and LED track car of C01-C are ABS. These statements do not specify the materials of every accessory, coating, ball or bell.

Do A01-T and A03-T include the same balls and bells?

A01-T lists 7 wooden balls. A03-T lists 5 iron balls and 6 bells. These are the recorded contents for the specified models. Confirm the sample and corresponding packaging for your order.
