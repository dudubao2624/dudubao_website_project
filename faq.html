What Is Your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

For DUDUBAO-branded stock, small orders are supported subject to actual inventory. Custom and private-label orders start at 500 sets per SKU.

Special packaging, dedicated accessories, or custom requirements may require a higher MOQ, confirmed during project review.

What Makes DUDUBAO Different From Other Magnetic Toy Suppliers?

DUDUBAO offers more than established magnetic tile products. We focus on original design, product innovation, and experienced quality control.

By combining magnetic toy development expertise with OEM/ODM capabilities, we help international toy brands create differentiated and competitive product ranges.

Our goal is to become a reliable long-term product partner, not simply fulfill individual orders.

Do You Offer OEM and ODM Services?

Yes.

We offer customization options including private-label logos, packaging design, product colors, set configurations, and selected accessories.

We also welcome discussions about innovative structures, functional components, and new product development.

Whether you are expanding an existing product range or exploring a new concept, we can work with you to evaluate suitable development solutions.

Can You Develop Custom Products for Our Brand?

Discuss custom product development, subject to feasibility, testing, tooling, commercial terms, and IP review.

With a focus on original design and product innovation, DUDUBAO can explore new structures, functional accessories, custom sets, and packaging concepts. Market availability is confirmed for each project.

Specific exclusivity arrangements, tooling ownership, and intellectual property rights can be discussed and agreed upon based on project requirements and commercial terms.

What Magnetic Toy Systems Does DUDUBAO Offer?

Our product directions include standard magnetic tiles, mini magnetic tiles, magnetic ball runs, interactive accessories, and innovative magnetic construction concepts.

These options help toy brands build broader product ranges for different play styles and market positions.

Specific product configurations and compatibility between products can be confirmed based on the selected models.

What International Testing and Compliance Support Do You Offer?

Send the selected SKU, destination and the document questions raised by your buyer or reviewer. Use the safety testing guide to prepare a focused request covering product identity, document scope and open points. Documents are matched to SKU and destination per project.
