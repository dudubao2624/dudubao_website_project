What DUDUBAO does

  • DUDUBAO is a magnetic toy manufacturer offering OEM, private-label and project-based ODM services.
  • DUDUBAO works with brands, distributors and retailers on magnetic toy sourcing projects.

Product lines

  • The DUDUBAO range includes standard magnetic tiles, MINI magnetic tiles, tube ball runs, bell play systems, car tracks, construction vehicle sets and selected accessories.
  • MINI magnetic tiles form a separate size branch, with compatibility confirmed for the selected products.

Customization scope

  • OEM options for selected DUDUBAO products include private-label logo and artwork, packaging format and design, set configurations, custom colors and selected existing accessories.
  • More complex ODM projects are assessed separately.
  • New accessories, structures, and tooling or molds require feasibility, target-market, testing, quantity, commercial, and IP review.
